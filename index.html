The practical details

Questions you're about to ask.

Who should come from our brand?

The founder and whoever runs creative or growth. Two seats per brand, three if we pair you with an AI expert.

We've barely used AI. Is this for us?

Yes. Tell us at registration and we'll pair an AI expert at your table. Mentors are on the floor all day either way.

Can we prepare in advance?

Think, don't build. You get your track and a problem prompt the day before. Platform access opens on the morning, every workspace is logged, and pre-built work is disqualified.

What happens in the CXO room?

A closed-door session for founders and CXOs with consumer-tech and AI leaders. Off the record, no slides, any question goes.

How is it judged?

Each track has one outcome you prove on screen: thumb-stops, buy taps, or assets from a cold run. The full scoring is in the handbook.
