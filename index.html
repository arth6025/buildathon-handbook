The problem

Most brands rent creative. It stops when the agency does.

Every campaign in 2026 meant a new brief, a new wait and a new invoice. The ads that worked were made once and never made again, because nobody could say exactly why they worked.

The top 1% of brands don't brief harder. They build a workflow: one that knows the brand, reads what customers actually say, and turns any product into a tested batch of ads without starting from zero.

The practical details

Questions you're about to ask.

Who should come from our brand?

Two people: the founder, and whoever runs creative or growth. The founder joins the CXO track at lunch while the operator keeps building.

Do we need anyone technical?

No. Sloosh is a creative canvas, not code, and the mentors are on the floor all day. The only setup is adding your BytePlus key to Sloosh the week before, and we help with that.

What do we bring?

Your brand kit, your top 3 ads, your reviews and 2 product links, sent three days before. One product to build on, one for the re-run.

Can we prepare work in advance?

Read the handbook as much as you like. But every workspace starts empty on the morning, and work built before the day is disqualified.

How is it judged?

Repeats 30 · on-brand 25 · converts 20 · credit-efficient 15 · ready to ship 10. False product claims are out. The full rubric is in the handbook.
