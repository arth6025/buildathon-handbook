02 / Mentors and experts

Matched by track and recipe.

Mentors run pods of 4 teams on the same track, grouped by recipe where possible, so one person sees four versions of the same engine and can unblock fast. Pod sizes follow sign-ups; a likely split is 2–3 pods on Virality, 2 on Revenue and 2 on Your AI Agency.

Floor mentor (7)

  • Owns one pod of 4 teams all day
  • Runs the T-1 call with each of them
  • Stamps every check-in
  • Checks the gates: on-brand, no false claims
  • Points; never takes the keyboard

Paired AI expert (10)

  • Sits with one assisted team all day
  • Sets up Claude + the Sloosh MCP and BYOK with them
  • Teaches the recipe; the brand drives
  • Flags fair-play doubts to the mentor

Experts in the CXO room (BytePlus, Shopify or Meta, Saif from Kovra group) also sit on the final panel.

04 / Submission

One form, one window, no extensions.

Modelled on the GrowthX rule: if a judge can't open it from their own device, it doesn't count. The window opens at 15:30 and closes at 16:00. Late is not judged.

1
Team, track, recipePicked from a list, so scoring sorts itself.
2
The Sloosh Project linkFinal assets marked Approved. Rejected ones stay for the audit.
3
The Claude share linkThe conversation that ran the MCP chain. This is the engine, not the output.
4
The angle in one sentenceNo product name in it.
5
Track extrasVirality: three ads for the Feed Test. Revenue: ads for the Feed Test plus the rival listing for the head-to-head. Your AI Agency: the engine ready for a cold run.

Attached automatically: the workspace log and the Project's CSV export. Engineering flags anything made before 10:15 or imported from outside.

05 / Judging

Two rounds. The room picks five; the panel picks one.

Round 1 · everyone (16:00–16:45)

  • Root parameter by track: thumb-stops (Virality), buy taps and head-to-head wins (Revenue), approved assets from a cold run (Your AI Agency). Overflow uncapped.
  • Power-ups: +10 each for a regional version, a video cut, the curveball cleared, under credit budget.
  • Gates, checked by mentors and the logs: off-brand assets don't count; a false claim or a fair-play miss is out.

The top 5

The five highest Round 1 scores, with at least one team from each track. If a track has no team in the top five, its best team takes the fifth seat.
