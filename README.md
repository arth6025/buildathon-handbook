## What this is

One invite-only day where **30+ consumer brands (₹1Cr+)** each build the AI creative engine they'll run all of 2027, from an empty canvas. AI experts sit with the teams that want one, agency owners mentor the floor, and founders get a closed-door CXO room.

> **Not a talk. A build.** 2026 ran on agencies. 2027 runs on you.

## Who's in the room (60–75 people)

Every team is one brand plus one AI expert: **a team of 2** (founder or operator + AI expert) or **a team of 3** (founder + operator + AI expert). One mentor for every 4 teams. The plan at 72 people: 26 teams of 2 + 4 teams of 3 = **30 brands, 30 AI experts, 8 mentors**. Sizing for 60–75 is in the [run sheet](https://arth6025.github.io/buildathon-handbook/flow/run-sheet/) (internal).

---|---|---|---|
| Brands already using AI | **Brand duo:** founder + operator | 18 | 36 |
| Brands new to AI | **Assisted pair:** operator + paired AI expert | 10 | 20 |
| Floor mentors | Agency owners: AI + creative experts, 1 for every 4 teams | 7 | 7 |
| The panel | Consumer-tech + AI leaders who judge the top 5 | 4 | 4 |

Teams are 2 or 3 people, never more. Who gets an AI expert comes from a 3-question AI-comfort check at registration.

## The three tracks: pick one outcome, prove it

| Track | What teams build | Root parameter | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **01 Virality** | Statics, carousels, hooks, UGC-style video, regional cuts | Would a stranger stop (40) · the angle (30) · on-brand (30) | 100, scored by mentors |
| **02 Revenue** | A personalised funnel (ad → landing page → email) on Sloosh; listings, rival counter-ads | One promise ad → page → email (40) · offer + proof (30) · on-brand (30) | 100, scored by mentors |
| **03 Your AI Agency** | Replace the shoot, the catalogue or the rollout | Runs on a new product (40) · approved assets (30) · on-brand (30) | 100, scored by mentors |

**Gates:** off-brand assets don't count · a false claim means you're out · failing fair play means you're out.
**Power-ups (+10 each):** a regional-language version · a video cut · the 14:00 curveball cleared · finishing under the credit budget.
**Judging:** mentors score every team on its track, with the build logs open. No audience vote. The best work goes in front of the public after the day, as a showcase.

### The CXO room

A closed-door table for founders and CXOs while their teams build. Leaders from platforms like Meta, Shopify and BytePlus, plus AI and consumer operators, open the playbook on how the world's best consumer brands use AI. Off the record, no slides, nothing off limits: budgets, building, hiring, shipping, what flopped.

### Fair play

Platform access opens at 10:15 on the day. Every workspace is logged from its first click. Pre-built workflows, prompts or assets, or work deployed from elsewhere, mean disqualification. Monitoring: **Baisakhi, Manish and engineering.**

## The 128 cards

The ShopOS × DSG deck, on screen. Each card names one problem; flip it for the build that fixes it. Filter by section, search by brand.

- **Every card names a brand.** All 128 are matched to one of the **70 live DSG portfolio companies** (Active or Partially Exited) — every one above **₹1 Cr** revenue. All 70 are used; no card is generic.
- **Brand and problem are both chosen to fit the build on the back.** A card whose front doesn't match its back is worse than no card, so the build decides who it's addressed to — the scrubs lookbook goes to Knya, the mattress film to SleepyCat.
- Each card keeps its original build side: build name, three steps, inputs, metric, outputs.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Read the brand-named cards as conversation starters, not findings.** Each problem is inferred from the brand's category and its build — **not** from an audit of that company's store or ad account. The framing is conditional on purpose: *"Flip this over if your brand has this problem."* Review before using them anywhere outside the room.

**Sections and colours** — straight from the print deck:

`Food & drinks` 18 · `Beauty & personal care` 14 · `Health & wellness` 10 · `Fashion & accessories` 10 · `Home, kids & pets` 12 · `Cafés & restaurants` 12 · `Stays, travel & experiences` 8 · `Every brand` 16 · `Shopify pages` 28

## What brands can build

Seeds, not specs. Each fits one Orbit track, is small enough to build in a day, and is useful enough that a brand would keep running it through 2027.

| Idea | What it does | Track |
|---|---|---|
| **Ad engine from your winners** | One input: your top 3 ads. Finds what made them work and returns 12+ statics ready for Ads Manager. | 01 |
| **Creative refresh loop** | Watches which ads are fatiguing, generates iterations of the winner rather than fresh concepts from zero. | 01 |
| **Hook tester** | Twenty openings against one angle, each stress-tested as a skeptical media buyer. | 01 |
| **Occasion switcher** | Re-stages one winning static for every moment in your 2027 calendar, with an argument that survives the discount being removed. | 01 |
| **Rival rebuild** | Names the pattern and angle in a competitor's longest-running ad, then rebuilds the angle on your product with your own proof. | 02 |
| **Category watch** | Pulls what rivals claim this month and returns the three claims nobody in the category is making, as ready-to-test statics. | 02 |
| **Review-to-video pipeline** | Turns the verbatim language in your reviews into UGC-style scripts and Seedance videos. | 01 |
| **Proof explainer** | Answers the one objection in your comments with a 15-second video and a 6-second cut. | 01 |

**The shape that wins is consistent:** narrowest possible input, output so complete it needs no second tool.

## Repo structure

```
├── index.html              THE PITCH — the front door
├── preview/                every page in one link, with device frames
├── handbook/               the builder handbook: 10 short chapters, one file
├── playbook/               the full 15-section reference (swipe files, prompts, briefs)
├── cards/                  the 128 cards, standalone
│
├── assets/
│   ├── sloosh.css          ← SLOOSH brand tokens. Link FIRST on every page.
│   ├── cards.css           card components, shared by /cards/ and the handbook
│   ├── cardgrid.js         CardGrid.mount(root, CARDS) — shared card renderer
│   ├── pitch.css           pitch styling
│   └── styles.css          handbook styling
│
├── data/                   ← ALL CONTENT LIVES HERE
│   ├── cards.js            the 128 cards
│   ├── swipe.js            the 52 swipe-file patterns
│   ├── prompts.js          prompts, briefs, launch-post skeleton
│   └── handbook.js         SECTIONS, teardown protocol, scoring, build ideas
│
└── app.js                  handbook renderer — no dependencies
```

**No build step, no dependencies, no framework.** Content is separated from rendering: you never touch `app.js` to add a pattern, a prompt, a card or a whole section.

### Branding

The site uses the **Sloosh** identity from the ShopOS × DSG print deck — near-black `#090909`, yellow `#FECC15`, the googly-eye wordmark, thick black rules and offset shadows, plus the nine section colours. All tokens live in [`assets/sloosh.css`](assets/sloosh.css) and are linked before every page stylesheet, so changing a colour in one place changes it everywhere.

## On sourcing

Benchmarks in the handbook are aggregated from public marketing write-ups and vendor blogs — **not primary research**. Most are single-test or single-account results.

Use them to decide what to test first, not as facts to quote on stage. Meta publishes no official hook-rate benchmark; your own account history is the only one that binds.
