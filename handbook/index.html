    03 / Rules

    The rules.

    01
    Two people per brand.The founder, and whoever runs creative or growth. Both registered, both in the room.
    02
    Pick one track.A, B or C. You lock it at check-in. One rubric scores every track.
    03
    Build in Sloosh, on your credits.Images on Seedream, video on Seedance, paid from your own BytePlus key.
    04
    Build from zero, on the day.Every workspace starts empty at 10:15. Sloosh's version history is the audit trail.
    05
    The keyboard stays with you.Mentors point, you click. That's how the skill stays in your team.
    06
    No false claims.A product claim you can't back on any asset means you're out, whatever else you scored.
    07
    Lock by 16:30.Late work is not judged, whatever the reason.

    • This handbook and the playbook

    Not allowed

    • Workflows or prompts built before the day

    04 / Scoring

    How you're scored.

    One rubric for all three tracks. It rewards a workflow that repeats over one beautiful asset.

    CriterionPointsHow it's judged
    Repeats30Judges hand you a second product and your workflow runs on it live, with no edits. A one-off asset scores zero here.
    On-brand25Would the brand run it tomorrow, without a designer fixing it first?
    Converts20A real angle in one sentence, a hook that earns the next 1.5 seconds, claims backed by proof.
    Credit-efficient15BytePlus credits spent per usable asset. Sloosh logs it.
    Ready to ship10Sized and labelled, each variant tagged with the one thing it changes.

    The test that decides most results: can your workflow run again on a product it has never seen? Practise that before 15:30.

    Common breaks and fixes.

    Key rejected
    Check the key is pasted in full, with no spaces. Still failing? Raise a flag at check-in.
    No balance
    The credits haven't been claimed yet on BytePlus. Do step 1 in chapter 02 again.
    Output looks generic
    Feed it your reviews and your top ads, not just the product page. Customer words beat model words.
    Off-brand colours
    Reload the brand kit and say in the prompt which colours to use.

    06 / Pick a track

    Pick one track. Build one workflow.

    Track A · the default

    Creative Velocity

    Take apart your top 3 ads, find what made them work, and build 12+ new statics from it. Seedream.

    Track B

    Competitor → Creative

    Take apart a competitor's best ad, then rebuild the angle on your product with your own proof. Seedream.

    Track C

    Video Ads

    Turn your reviews into scripts, then short UGC-style videos. Seedance.

    Every track, same four steps.

    1 · Input
    One product, plus your ads or a competitor's.
    2 · Context
    Your brand kit and your customers' own words from reviews.
    3 · Generate
    12 angles → keep 4 → 20 hooks → keep 3 → 3 hooks × 4 sizes = 12 ads.
    4 · Re-run
    Run the same workflow on your second product. No edits.

    Not sure? Pick A. It works for every brand with ads already running.
