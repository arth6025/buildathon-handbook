      03 / Rules

      The rules.

      01
      Teams of 2 or 3.Founder and operator, plus a paired AI expert if you asked for one. Never more than three.
      02
      Pick one track.01, 02 or 03. It locks at check-in, and that track's rubric is how you score.
      03
      Build in Sloosh, on the day.Sloosh workflows for the pieces, the Sloosh MCP to orchestrate them. Access opens at 10:15 on 16 Dec. Not before.
      04
      Fair play.No pre-built workflows, prompts or assets. Everything you score on is made in the room.
      05
      The keyboard stays with the brand.Experts and mentors guide. Your team drives.
      06
      No false claims.One product claim you can't back, on any asset, and you're out.
      07
      Lock by 16:00.The Feed Test starts at 16:00. Late work isn't in it.

      Fair play: build it here, build it today.

      Bring

      • Your brand kit, ads, reviews and product links
      • Your problem, written down the night before
      • Notes and sketches

      Don't bring

      • Workflows or prompts built before the day
      • Assets made elsewhere, deployed in the room
      • An agency or freelancer building for you

      Every workspace is logged from its first click: what was made, when and by whom. Work imported from outside is flagged to the judges before scoring. Unsure? Ask a mentor first.

      04 / Scoring

      How you're scored.

      One outcome per track, proven on screen. Hit the base, then keep going: overflow is uncapped.

      TrackRoot parameterPoints
      01 Stop the ScrollThumb-stops in the Feed Test (a pause of 1.5 seconds or more), 10×150 base
      02 Make Them BuyBuy taps in the Feed Test plus blind head-to-head wins against your top rival, 20×200 base
      03 Your Own Creative EngineApproved assets from the cold run on a product you've never seen, 5×150 base

      The gates

      • Off-brand doesn't count. An asset the brand wouldn't run adds nothing to your root parameter.
      • A false claim means you're out.
      • Failing fair play means you're out.

      Power-ups (+10 each)

      • A regional-language version that's rewritten, not translated
      • A video cut of your best static
      • The 14:00 curveball, cleared
      • Finishing under your credit budget

      Cross-track bonus: results outside your track count as bonus points. Your main rubric stays locked.

      Common breaks and fixes.

      Can't log in
      Use the email you registered with. Still stuck? Raise a flag at check-in.
      Out of credits
      Tell a mentor. And check you're not regenerating the same asset ten times.
      Output looks generic
      Feed it your reviews and your top ads, not just the product page. Customer words beat model words.
      Off-brand colours
      Reload the brand kit and name the colours in the prompt.

      06 / Pick a track

      Pick one outcome. Prove it.

      Three tracks. You pick one at registration, and that's the rubric you're scored on.

      Track 01

      Stop the Scroll

      Creative people can't scroll past: statics, carousels, hooks, UGC-style video, regional cuts.

      Root parameter: thumb-stops, 10×. 150 base, overflow uncapped.

      Track 02 · hardest

      Make Them Buy

      Turn attention into a click or a cart: listings, quick-commerce tiles, rival counter-ads, lifecycle creative.

      Root parameter: buy taps and head-to-head wins, 20×. 200 base, overflow uncapped.

      Track 03

      Your Own Creative Engine

      Replace a creative function: the shoot, the catalogue, the campaign rollout across every surface.

      Root parameter: approved assets from the cold run, 5×. 150 base, overflow uncapped.

      Every track, same four steps.

      1 · Input
      One product, plus your ads or a rival's.
      2 · Context
      Your brand kit and your customers' own words from reviews.
      3 · Generate
      12 angles → keep 4 → 20 hooks → keep 3 → make the batch.
      + Orchestrate
      Chain your Sloosh workflows through the Sloosh MCP, so one engine runs them all.
      4 · Re-run
      Same engine, second product, no edits.

      How to choose: pick the track where your team can show the strongest proof by 16:00. Ads running already? Start with 01.
