      04 / Scoring

      How you're scored.

      Your track's rubric, scored by mentors with the build logs open: the outcome, the quality of the output, your case and on-brand. No audience vote.

      Rubric 101: L1 to L5

      Mentors score four parameters on a level. Each level is worth a quarter more: L1 = 0 · L2 = 25% · L3 = 50% · L4 = 75% · L5 = all the points. 100 points in all, and the quality of what you make counts as much as the result.

      • The build log. Every workspace is logged from its first click at 10:15 AM. Anything made before, or imported from outside, doesn't count.

      • The claims. Mentors check every product claim. One false claim zeroes your score.

      Common breaks and fixes.

      Can't log in
      Use the email you registered with. Still stuck? Raise a flag at check-in.
      Out of credits
      Tell a mentor. And check you're not regenerating the same asset ten times.
      Output looks generic
      Feed it your reviews and your top ads, not just the product page. Customer words beat model words.
      Off-brand colours
      Reload the brand kit and name the colours in the prompt.

      06 / Pick a track

      Two tracks. Pick yours.

      The brand track is for brand teams: pick Visibility or Revenue. The builder track is for external AI builders: build an AI agency in front of the 20–30 CXOs in the room, who are hiring.

      Brand track · 01

      Visibility

      Creative people can't scroll past: statics, carousels, hooks, UGC-style video, regional cuts.

      Outcome scored: a stranger stops scrolling.

      Brand track · 02

      Revenue

      Turn attention into a click or a cart: listings, quick-commerce tiles, rival counter-ads, lifecycle creative.

      Outcome scored: one promise, ad → page → email.

      Builder track · 03

      AI Agency

      A team of AI agents that does an agency's job: the shoot, the catalogue, the campaign rollout across every surface.

      Outcome scored: runs on a product it has never seen.

      Every track, same four steps.

      1 · Input
      One product, plus your ads or a rival's.
      2 · Context
      Your brand kit and your customers' own words from reviews.
      3 · Generate
      12 angles → keep 4 → 20 hooks → keep 3 → make the batch.
      + Orchestrate
      Chain your Sloosh workflows through the Sloosh MCP, so one engine runs them all.
      4 · Re-run
      Same engine, second product, no edits.

      Brand track: pick an idea from the library or bring your own, and make the case for why it will work for your brand. That case is your angle, and it's 30 of your 100 points. Builder track: pitch the agency you'd build, then build it.
